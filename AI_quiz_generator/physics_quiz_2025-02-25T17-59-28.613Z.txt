Question 1: Which of the following is NOT a state of matter?
A) Solid
B) Liquid
C) Force
D) Gas
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Solids, liquids, and gases are the three main states of matter. Force is a push or a pull.

Question 2: What happens to the temperature of matter when its particles slow down?
A) It increases
B) It decreases
C) It stays the same
D) It fluctuates randomly
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: Temperature measures how hot or cold matter is. When particles slow down, the matter has less thermal energy, resulting in a lower temperature.

Question 3: Two opposing forces acting on an object have different magnitudes. What can be said about the net force on the object?
A) The net force is zero.
B) The net force is balanced.
C) The net force is unbalanced.
D) The net force cannot be determined.
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: When opposing forces have different magnitudes, they are unbalanced, resulting in a net force acting on the object.

Question 4:  If an object moves higher above the Earth's surface (further from the center of Earth), what happens to its gravitational potential energy?
A) It increases
B) It decreases
C) It stays the same
D) It depends on the object's mass
Correct Answer: A
Explanation: Gravitational potential energy increases as the distance between the object and the center of the Earth increases.

Question 5: A car travels 100 miles in 2 hours. What is its speed?
A) 200 miles per hour
B) 100 miles per hour
C) 50 miles per hour
D) 25 miles per hour
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Speed is calculated by dividing distance by time.  100 miles / 2 hours = 50 miles per hour.
