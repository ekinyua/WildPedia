Question 1: What happens to the gravitational potential energy between an object and Earth when the object's mass remains constant but its distance from Earth's center increases?
A) It decreases
B) It stays the same
C) It increases
D) It fluctuates

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Gravitational potential energy increases as the distance between two objects increases.  The lecture notes explicitly state, "If the distance between an object and the center of Earth increases, the gravitational potential energy stored between the object and Earth increases."

Question 2: Which of the following is NOT a state of matter?
A) Solid
B) Liquid
C) Force
D) Gas

Correct Answer: C
Explanation: Solids, liquids, and gases are the states of matter discussed in the lecture. Force is a push or a pull, a different concept.

Question 3:  If two opposing forces act on an object and they have different magnitudes, the forces are:
A) Balanced
B) Unbalanced
C) Neutral
D) Equivalent

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The lecture notes define unbalanced forces as those with different magnitudes acting in opposite directions.

Question 4:  Two bricks, one weighing 1 kilogram and the other 2 kilograms, are both at 70°F. Which statement about their thermal energy is true?
A) They have the same amount of thermal energy.
B) The 1-kilogram brick has twice the thermal energy of the 2-kilogram brick.
C) The 2-kilogram brick has twice the thermal energy of the 1-kilogram brick.
D) Thermal energy is unrelated to mass.

Correct Answer: C
Explanation:  The lecture explains that thermal energy depends on the amount of matter. Since the 2-kilogram brick has twice the mass, it has twice the thermal energy, even at the same temperature.

Question 5:  A liquid's shape is determined by:
A) Its inherent structure
B) The shape of its container
C) Its volume
D) The particles it's made of

Correct Answer: B
Explanation: As stated in the lecture, a liquid takes the shape of its container.
