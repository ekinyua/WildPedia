Question 1: What is matter?
A) Anything that has volume
B) Anything that takes up space
C) Anything that has mass
D) Anything that has weight
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The lecture notes define matter as anything that takes up space.

Question 2: Which of the following is NOT a state of matter described in the lecture?
A) Solid
B) Liquid
C) Plasma
D) Gas
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The lecture notes discuss solids, liquids, and gases as states of matter. Plasma is another state of matter, but it is not mentioned in these notes.

Question 3: What happens to the gravitational potential energy between an object and Earth when the object is raised to a higher position?
A) It decreases
B) It increases
C) It stays the same
D) It depends on the object's mass
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: According to the lecture notes, the gravitational potential energy increases when the distance between the object and Earth's center increases.

Question 4: What is thermal energy?
A) The energy stored in an object due to its position
B) The energy of moving atoms
C) The energy an object has due to its motion
D) The energy stored in an object due to its temperature
Correct Answer: B
Explanation: The lecture notes define thermal energy as the energy of moving atoms.

Question 5: If two opposing forces act on an object with different magnitudes, what can be said about the net force?
A) There is no net force
B) The net force is balanced
C) The net force is unbalanced
D) The net force is zero
Correct Answer: C
Explanation: The lecture notes state that when opposing forces have different magnitudes, the forces are unbalanced, resulting in a net force on the object.
